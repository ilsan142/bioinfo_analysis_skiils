# Sources for manuscript communication guidance

Checked on 2026-10-21. These primary communication guides were inspected directly. The skill uses original wording and domain-specific safeguards; it does not reproduce their text, diagrams, or annotated papers. The pages identify their CommKit content as licensed under Creative Commons Attribution Non-Commercial 4.0 unless otherwise noted. Consult the source and its terms before reusing its material.

Read this note when checking the origin of the writing guidance or adapting it to a journal. Routine drafting does not require opening every source. These are writing references, not citations supporting biological claims in a manuscript.

| Source | Principle informing this skill | Application boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [MIT Broad Research Communication Lab: Journal Article — Results](https://mitcommlab.mit.edu/broad/commkit/journal-article-results/) | Arrange findings into a readable progression; explain the purpose of each comparison, the observations, and the reason for the next step. Keep the evidence visible enough for readers to assess the conclusion. | Reordering exposition must not rewrite the history of hypothesis formation. Relevance to a claim, including contradictory evidence, determines inclusion; significance does not determine which findings deserve disclosure. |
| [MIT Broad Research Communication Lab: Journal Article — Methods](https://mitcommlab.mit.edu/broad/commkit/journal-article-methods/) | Explain methodological purpose and supply the particulars needed to assess and repeat the work. Make corresponding procedures easy to locate. | Actual records govern the description. Shared headings, writing sequence, and grammatical voice are optional choices, not requirements. A standard-method citation cannot fill unknown study-specific settings. |
| [MIT Biological Engineering Communication Lab: Journal Article — Discussion](https://mitcommlab.mit.edu/be/commkit/journal-article-discussion/) | Relate the study's contribution to its opening question, prior evidence, unresolved issues, and useful future directions. | Interpretive breadth must remain bounded by measurement and design. Literature context and future work must not turn speculation into an observed finding or validation. |

The Broad Results and Methods pages acknowledge adaptation from the MIT Biological Engineering Communication Lab. The Discussion page records a February 2026 update. These dates and provenance details describe the inspected pages, not a guarantee that they remain unchanged.

The evidence mapping, run reconciliation, correction handling, replicate accounting, and executed-versus-proposed distinctions in this skill are original bioinformatics adaptations. They are not presented as quotations or universal rules issued by MIT.
